Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a traditional Italian baked pasta dish made of wide, flat sheets of pasta layered alternately with savory fillings, sauces, and cheese, then baked al forno (in the oven).

Ingredients

  • Pasta Sheets
  • Ragù (Meat Sauce)
  • Béchamel Sauce (Besciamella)
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Make the Meat Ragù
  2. Make the Béchamel Sauce
  3. Prepare the Pasta Sheets
  4. Assemble the Lasagna
  5. Bake and Rest
