Pizza margherita

Description

A Pizza Margherita is a traditional Neapolitan-style thin-crust pizza topped with crushed tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, fresh basil, and extra virgin olive oil, matching the red, white, and green colors of the Italian flag.

Ingredients

  • Fresh dough
  • Peeled tomatoes
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Basil
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Make and Proof the Dough
  2. Prepare the Sauce
  3. Shape the Base
  4. Assemble the Pizza
  5. Bake
  6. Garnish and Serve
