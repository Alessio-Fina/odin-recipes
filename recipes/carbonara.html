Pasta carbonara

Description

Pasta carbonara is a classic Roman pasta dish made with long noodles, cured pork, hard cheese, eggs, and black pepper.

Ingredients

  • Guanciale
  • Eggs
  • Cheese
  • Black Pepper
  • Pasta

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta
  2. Render the guanciale
  3. Make the egg mixture
  4. Combine off the heat
  5. Emulsify the sauce
  6. Serve
